出典の文面

Bring to a temperature of 80–85°C — small bubbles forming at the base ... but no rolling simmer. At 100°C, the turbulence tears the white apart before it sets. At 70°C, the white never fully coagulates. / The cooking time (3–4 minutes) is chosen to bring the white to full coagulation while keeping the center of the yolk below 65°C（terumimorita）／「沸騰させると水の対流が生じ白身が崩れてしまう」「85度を目安に…五分間」（travelingfoodlab）／The egg is cooked at 80C for 4 minutes（soc_poach）
